## What Outstaff is

Outstaff is an open-source TypeScript framework for running a team of AI agents like an outsourcing agency. You describe a team in a file in your own repository. Outstaff runs the people on that team, hands them work from your tracker, and keeps the work moving through your code host and chat.

## The agency analogy

An agency keeps a catalog of roles it can staff, signs a contract with a client, assembles a team for that client, and runs an office that tracks every piece of work.

<CardGrid>

## Status

Outstaff is in early development. There is no stable release, the APIs will change, and many pages in this site are placeholders. Sections marked "coming soon" describe what will be documented as each feature lands.
